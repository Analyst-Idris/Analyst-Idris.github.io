Who I Am

I am Idris Oladejo, a Data Analyst and Machine Learning Engineer with hands-on experience building end-to-end predictive analytics solutions, business intelligence dashboards, and data-driven decision-support tools — based at Data Mentor Hub.

I am proficient in Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn), supervised machine learning (regression, classification), model evaluation, and deployment using Streamlit. I also bring a strong foundation in SQL, Power BI (DAX, Power Query), and Microsoft Excel for BI and analytics reporting — plus R (ggplot2, dplyr, caret) and SPSS for statistical work.

As an experienced Data Analysis Instructor, I develop curricula, mentor aspiring analysts, and communicate complex findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. I am committed to solving real-world business problems through machine learning, statistical analysis, and actionable data storytelling.
